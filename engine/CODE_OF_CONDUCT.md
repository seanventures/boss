# BOSS Engine Code of Conduct

We expect participants to be respectful, constructive, and professional.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, publishing another
person's private information, and disruptive bad-faith behavior are not
acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other
proportionate action to protect contributors and users. Reports should include
the relevant context, dates, and any available evidence; avoid including
credentials or sensitive customer data.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **boss@sean.net**. Design partners may
also use the private contact supplied with repository access. For suspected
security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
