# Code of Conduct

BOSS is built in the open by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We want every issue, pull request, discussion, and other project space to be a place where people can do their best work.

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and professional. Assume good intent, give specific and actionable feedback, and credit the work of others.

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, or threats
- personal attacks and insults
- sharing someone else's private information without permission
- repeated disruptive behavior after being asked to stop

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect contributors and users. They consider context and the impact of the behavior when responding.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to [boss@sean.net](mailto:boss@sean.net). Include links, dates, and relevant context, but no credentials or customer data. Reports are handled confidentially.

For a suspected vulnerability, use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).
