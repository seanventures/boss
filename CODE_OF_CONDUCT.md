# BOSS Community Code of Conduct

We expect people in BOSS issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces to be respectful, constructive, and professional. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sharing someone else's private information, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action to protect contributors and users. They will consider context and the impact of the behavior when responding.

Report conduct concerns privately to [boss@sean.net](mailto:boss@sean.net). Include links, dates, and relevant context, but do not include credentials or customer data. For a suspected vulnerability, use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).
