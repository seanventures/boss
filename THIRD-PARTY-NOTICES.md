These files are versioned source-level notice inputs for the Community export and container build; they are **not** a complete image inventory, source offer, or legal clearance. The final release images and optional install profiles still require independent review.

The source tarballs and upstream tag were checked against the pinned versions in `pnpm-lock.yaml` and the previously inventoried global npm tree. SHA-256 pins in `scripts/community_export_manifest.json` make the Community exporter reject an edited or missing notice. Those checks do not establish which package variants or native libraries are present in a rebuilt x86 or ARM64 image. They also do not dispose of FFmpeg, Psycopg binary, Playwright Chromium, or coding CLI notices and terms.
