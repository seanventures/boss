Design notes: this page is only the list
  1. 1 Govern › Autonomy becomes Where people decide. The 7 cards are the compliance boundary: production deploy, merge to the main branch, spend above the cap, secrets, messages outside the team, deleting data, and who can do what. The 8th, changing this list, is fixed. Anything not on a card, agents do without asking. Each card shows its rule, who decides, how often it came up today, and why in one line. A click opens its settings and audit trail. A card can make a rule stricter, never looser (enforced in code).
  2. 2 To change a rule, type it in plain words, the same one-input pattern as Add a project.
  3. 3 Rule change shows each edit to a card, flags what is already allowed, and replays the last 30 days so the effect is visible before you apply it. Applying needs your passkey, because changing the list is on the list.
  4. The one line at the top is the only activity here. What agents did is shown on Agents at work.
